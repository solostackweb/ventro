## Selection Criteria

1. **AI-native**: Core product is AI/ML (not just "AI-enabled")
2. **Recent activity**: ≥1 public announcement, launch, or funding in last 12 months (2025–2026)
3. **Source accessibility**: Official blog, RSS, GitHub, Hugging Face, or press page
4. **Category diversity**: Foundation models, infra/MLOps, applications, robotics, hardware, research
5. **Geographic diversity**: US, India, EU, Israel, Canada, UK represented
6. **Stage diversity**: Pre-seed through public companies
7. **YC representation**: Include recent YC AI batches (W24, S24, W25, S25)
