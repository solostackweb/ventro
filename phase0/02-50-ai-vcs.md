## Selection Criteria

1. **AI thesis explicit**: Fund has published AI/ML thesis or >30% portfolio in AI
2. **Recent activity**: ≥1 AI investment announced in last 12 months (2025–2026)
3. **Source accessibility**: Official blog, RSS, portfolio page, or partner Medium/Substack
4. **Geographic diversity**: US, EU, India, Israel, Canada, Singapore represented
5. **Stage coverage**: Pre-seed through growth represented
6. **No duplicates**: Distinct fund vehicles; partners tracked separately where thesis differs
